Questions before you join

Is New Lifestyle Way Gym open 24 hours?

Yes. The gym in Al Nahda, Sharjah is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Trainers are available around the clock too, so book a session for the hour that suits you.

Can I try the gym before I join?

Yes. A free demo includes a fitness assessment, a meeting with a potential trainer and a full tour of the facility. Use the booking form below or message us on WhatsApp.

Do you offer home personal training in Sharjah?

Yes. A certified trainer comes to your home with the equipment and builds sessions around your goal. It covers Al Nahda and the rest of the Sharjah emirate.

How much is a gym membership?

The price depends on the plan and how many personal training sessions you add. Message us on WhatsApp for today’s rates, or book a free demo and we will go through the options with you.

Can I train online?

Yes. Online coaching gives you live video sessions, a personal plan and real-time form correction, from Sharjah or anywhere else in the world.

Where is the gym?

Al Tayer Tower, G Floor, 5 19 Street, Hay Al Nahda, Sharjah, near Sahara Centre. We train members from Al Nahda, Al Qasimia, Bu Daniq and across Sharjah.
